## 📝 QUESTION 4: Phone Number
**Type:** Short Answer  
**Required:** YES ✓  
**Validation:** Kenyan phone format

**How to add:**
1. Click + button
2. Type: `Phone Number`
3. Click ⋮ → Response validation
4. Select: Text → Regular expression → Matches
5. Type pattern: `^(07|01)\d{8}$`
6. Helper text: `Enter Kenyan number (e.g., 0712345678)`
7. Toggle "Required" ON

## ✅ STEP 4: LINK TO GOOGLE SHEETS (2 minutes)

1. Click **"Responses"** tab at the top
2. Click the green **Google Sheets icon** 
3. Select **"Create a new spreadsheet"**
4. Name it: `JKUAT_Tech_Conference_Registrations`
5. Click **"Create"**

✅ Done! Responses now save automatically to Google Sheets

---

## 🎨 STEP 5: CUSTOMIZE THEME (2 minutes)

3. Select a professional font
4. Click outside to close

---

## ⚙️ STEP 6: CONFIGURE SETTINGS (2 minutes)

1. Click **gear icon** ⚙️ (top right)
2. Check these boxes:

   - ✅ Limit to 1 response

3. In "Confirmation message" paste:

## 🆘 QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING

**Can't find the + button?** → Look at the right side toolbar  
**Validation not working?** → Click ⋮ (three dots) → Response validation  
**Can't turn on Required?** → Look for toggle at bottom right of question  
**Can't link to Sheets?** → Make sure you're on "Responses" tab, not "Questions"
