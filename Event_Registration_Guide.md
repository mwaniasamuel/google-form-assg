## 🆘 TROUBLESHOOTING

**"I can't find the + button to add questions"**
→ Look at the right side of your screen - there's a floating toolbar with icons. The + icon is at the top.

**"My validation isn't working"**
→ Make sure you click the three dots (⋮) on the question → Response validation → Select the right type

**"Required toggle isn't turning on"**
→ Make sure you're clicking the actual toggle switch at the bottom right of the question box

**"I can't link to Google Sheets"**
→ Make sure you're on the "Responses" tab, not "Questions" tab

**"I made a mistake on a question"**
→ Just click on that question box and edit it. You can also drag questions to reorder them.

**"How do I delete a question?"**
→ Click the trash/delete icon in the top right of that question box
